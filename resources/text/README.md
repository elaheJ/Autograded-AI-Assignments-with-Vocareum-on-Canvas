These are the original portable text guidelines, retained for attachment or copying into an assistant. The formatted [Markdown guides](../../guidelines/START_HERE.md) are the primary reading path for this repository.

- [Start here and intake form](START_HERE.txt)
- [Prepare a notebook](01_CONVERT_NOTEBOOK_TO_VOCAREUM_AUTOGRADED.txt)
- [Deploy and verify](02_SET_UP_NOTEBOOK_ON_VOCAREUM.txt)
- [Assignment handoff template](03_ASSIGNMENT_HANDOFF_TEMPLATE.txt)

The general text guide recommends keeping private assessment answers out of public distributions. The repository's demo examples intentionally include instructor reference answers and grading checks for reuse under MIT; they are distinct from an institution's private course assessments. Publish the solved source through Vocareum and distribute its generated student release.
